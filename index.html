Clips Auto Live

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

A private Windows desktop tool used by one content creator (Splynter Raul) to turn recordings of his own live streams into edited episodes and short clips, and publish them to his own channels.

What it does

  • Watches the folder where OBS Studio saves the creator's own live-stream recordings.
  • Transcribes and edits the recordings locally (cuts dead time, builds chapters, renders episodes and Shorts).
  • Uploads the result to the creator's own YouTube channel with title, description, chapters, custom thumbnail and series playlist.

Access

The app is not publicly available or downloadable. It runs only on the creator's own computer and only accesses the creator's own accounts, authorized through each platform's official OAuth login. No server, no other users.

YouTube API usage

videos.insert, thumbnails.set, playlists.list, playlists.insert, playlistItems.insert, channels.list — own channel only.

Powered by YouTube API Services

Uploads to YouTube are made through the YouTube API Services, under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
